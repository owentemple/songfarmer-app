The buttons below will generate song seeds for you to get started working on a song.

The Setting button will generate a seed with a specific place in the world
and a time for you to begin to flow, or create ideas for a song.


The Type button will generate a seed that describes a type of song,
chosen from the 11 broad categories of songs that Bob Dylan writes.

(Here's an article with more details on each of the song types in the Dylan song classification.)



The Theme button will generate a seed that describes a value or theme,
chosen from the 24 character strengths and virtues identified by psychologists Martin Seligman and Chris Peterson.

(Here's an article with more details on each of the character strengths in the classification.)
